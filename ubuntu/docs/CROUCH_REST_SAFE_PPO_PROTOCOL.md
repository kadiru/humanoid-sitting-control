# Validated-surface crouch-to-rest PPO pilot

This is a new, bounded experiment. Completed studies remain unchanged. Ubuntu root: `/data/humanoid-sitting-perception/crouch_rest_bridge_01`; main study: `ppo_safe_surfaces_01`.

The force-balanced reference with flat-foot and seated hip feedback passes every active-tick slip, traction and contact check in both placements on six of the nine development geometries: 38 cm at −5/0/+5 degrees, 40 cm at 0/+5 degrees, and 42 cm at +5 degrees. The other three geometries remain failed development stress tests: 40 cm/−5 degrees, 42 cm/−5 degrees and 42 cm/0 degrees. No criteria are relaxed, and these failures must remain in the report. The older current reference/policies remain comparison baselines, not replaced policies.

The new controller preserves the old reference's first ten seconds, then uses depth/kinematics/IMU planning, predicted static joint preload, foot-flatness feedback and hip balance to transfer weight. Static robot inertial parameters are allowed model information; live simulator poses, geometry and contacts remain reset/reward/evaluation labels only. Explicit horizontal reference placement remains assistance. Actor and critic use 406 sensor/reference channels, with 23 bounded body-joint residuals; 14 finger commands remain fixed. Leg spans are 0.008–0.012 rad, torso 0.015 rad, arm spans 0.08 rad. The existing 0.003 rad/tick slew cap and joint limits remain.

Every episode starts physically standing. The learning/control window begins at 15 s, then may move to 13, 11 and 10 s. The first ten seconds never receive learned actions. Before each advance, the current checkpoint must pass a full 20 s evaluation at the proposed earlier window on all 12 admitted trials, with zero active-tick slip, traction-margin or illegal-contact violations and no physical failures. Merely passing a later window does not authorize earlier control. This replaces unsafe instantaneous resets into a moving crouch. It is progressive reference-assisted control, not discovery of the whole standing approach.

Training renders all 18 development rows to preserve the validated physics layout. The six excluded stress-test rows receive reference actions and are excluded from actor and critic updates, advantage normalization and KL statistics. This geometry membership is training sample selection only. Evaluation applies each learned policy to all conditions; there is no hidden truth-based runtime admission selector. Warmup and teacher-prefix samples are also excluded from learning. Masking is fixed for each policy decision and checked at every physics tick.

Seeds 58111 and 58117 start fresh, with zero-output actors. Each has four blocks of 16 scheduled rollout updates, 32 decisions/update, five physics ticks/decision, and 18 environments: 184,320 physics transitions/seed; 368,640 total. The first eight scheduled updates are critic-only; 56 are actor-enabled. Scheduled updates with no eligible samples may take no optimizer steps; report actual Adam steps and active samples separately. Initial global settling is excluded from this budget; indexed-reset warmup is included. Teacher, excluded-row and learned-control transitions must sum to active physics. Actor, critic and Adam state continue between blocks. No budget extensions or extra training.

Before the main study, a six-update optimizer smoke at the 15 s window uses 17,280 separate physics transitions. Six updates are needed because the first 15 s contain no eligible samples. Admission requires finite changed actor/critic weights, nonzero eligible control/optimization, no control on excluded rows, exact approach/startup matching, full-horizon evaluation, all 12 admitted cases passing strict validation, and no new physical-failure condition among the six stress trials. Main seeds do not inherit smoke weights. The physical reference regression must match the validated prototype exactly. Indexed-reset isolation must also pass after the controller has constructed its plan.

The reward remains the existing progressive-seat reward: during rest it rewards qualifying support and seat loading, penalizes total squared physical torque over all 37 joints, and penalizes friction use and physical failure. The 65% seat-load term is a soft target. The old study's 60% hard advancement threshold is not carried into this new pilot: the admitted physical reference spans about 49–73% seat load. Report this scope explicitly rather than claiming majority-weight support in every case. Stable support and clean movement remain hard validation gates; effort is compared afterward on common successes.

Eight geometries, each repeated at two placements, are reserved before optimizer smoke/training: 38.75/40.25/41.75 cm crossed with ±2 degrees, plus 40.75 cm at ±4 degrees. They are excluded from tuning, learning, calibration and selection. Both full-budget checkpoints and their control-window timing must freeze before any holdout evaluation. Holdout compares the current reference, current policies 56211/56217, the new reference, and both final PPO policies. Development reports include all nine geometries and separate admitted versus excluded conditions.

Report physical failures and stable/strict success first. Compare whole-20 s, final-10 s and final-5 s summed squared torque, summed absolute torque, instantaneous all-joint RMS averaged over time, and leg/upper-body contributions only on common full-duration successes. Separate reference-repair gains from PPO gains. Verify sources/prior hashes, finite changed weights, actual loaded checkpoints, optimizer continuity, all budget/mask accounting, startup matching, fixed fingers, bounded commands, actual control-window progression, and holdout separation. Audit geometry availability/error and foot-observer error before failures. Two seeds, repeated placements and a deliberately restricted training domain do not establish arbitrary-surface generalization. Torque is an effort proxy, not electrical energy or proof of a global optimum. No automatic policy promotion.
