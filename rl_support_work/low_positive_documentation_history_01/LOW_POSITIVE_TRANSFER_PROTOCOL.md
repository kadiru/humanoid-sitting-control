# Low positive-slope transfer — development protocol (not frozen)

Objective: eliminate remaining low-seat positive-slope slipping without unwanted contacts, retain established clean sitting cases, then compare a policy that controls a longer part of the motion against the previous repaired reference and policies. Positive slope descends toward the robot's feet.

Keep the original physical criteria and materials. Count unwanted contact as the union of forbidden-body contact and illegal support-patch contact. Report all-tick slip separately from conservative force-ratio warnings; the legacy ratio is not an actual friction-capacity certificate. Retain failed variants and stress cases. Truth is permitted for reset, reward and evaluation labels only, never runtime actor/critic/controller input.

Development includes the old24 development and old24 holdout conditions. Targeted12-trial repair bank covers36cm/+15,+20;37cm/+11.25,+16.25;40cm/+20;44cm/+15, each twice. Fresh24-trial holdout was reserved before simulations in reservation.json and stays closed through repair, normalization, training and checkpoint selection.

Before main training, compare each candidate with exact matched starts and the unchanged approach, then audit full20-second slip/contact/physical outcomes. Validate zero learned action including indexed resets, bounded random exploration, two-update optimizer smoke, CPU optimizer replay and deterministic matched smoke evaluation. Freeze sources, physical criteria, training bank, normalization, reward, budgets and selection before main launch. No post-freeze budget extension or threshold changes.

Proposed policy updates three symmetric sagittal residuals every.5s over a10–20s initial window; the frozen resting parent remains explicit. Inputs417 =414 sensors/reference plus3 previous residuals. Maximum new residual .004rad, slew .004rad/s; combined with parent at most.012rad. No fingers or other body-joint residuals. Reward uses complete, undiscounted remaining-episode total squared physical joint torque with explicit failure/support/slip/contact penalties. Action-independent development baselines center rewards; no holdout normalization or calibration. These are proposals pending physical preflight, not frozen main settings.

Use two seeds and a small fixed budget, defined before launch. Advance the control window earlier only after a prospective full-duration development test retains the admitted clean success set and effort. Report full-budget finals separately from development-selected checkpoints or explicit fallback. Evaluate both finals only after both checkpoints and selections are fixed.

Compare stability first, then whole20s/final10s/final5s total squared/absolute torque, average instantaneous all-joint RMS Nm, and leg/upper contributions on common full-duration clean successes. No savings from failed fragments.
