# Low-seat contact repair and gated PPO comparison

Authorized scope: reduce the remaining slipping and housing contacts on low positive-slope seats, preserve the current successful conditions, then run a small PPO comparison against the repaired reference and current policies. Positive slope descends toward the feet. Leaning and negative slopes are outside this pilot.

Completed studies are read-only. Preparation verified and protected 24,242 files. All previously evaluated surfaces are development evidence for this new study. A fresh bank of 24 trials (36.75/37.75/39.5/41.5 cm × +8.75/+13.75/+18.75 degrees, two placements) was reserved before new simulation and remains closed until both final policies and selections are fixed.

Recorded failure evidence: the 36 cm/+20 degree reference endpoint has about 0.6 mm predicted hip-housing clearance. The actual motion lands earlier than its planned path and oscillates at touchdown. This motivates a clearance change and added landing damping; it does not establish a single causal explanation. Earlier six manual repairs and the completed continuous-feedback PPO remain preserved, including all regressions.

Initial physical experiments compare two separately recorded candidates against exact same-GPU baselines: (1) damping of the existing path and (2) damping with a more forward-leaning, higher-clearance path. Damping uses encoders, IMU, the measured plane and fixed-foot kinematic anchors; simulator contacts and body poses are diagnostic labels only. Fixed-foot validity must be checked against physical foot-slip measurements. The first ten seconds and unselected surface plans remain unchanged. Geometric clearance is a model screen, not certification.

Each candidate and baseline runs all 24 probe conditions for the complete 20 seconds. The bank includes low-seat failures and established successful conditions. No physical thresholds, materials, support patches or evaluation rules are relaxed. Unwanted contact is the union of forbidden-body contact and illegal support-patch contact; conservative force-margin warnings are reported separately.

PPO launch requires a physically validated repair and regression comparison against current policies. The small training budget and exact learning definitions will be documented and frozen only after this prerequisite is met. No main training or fresh holdout is started by the probe launcher. No policy is promoted automatically.

Final comparisons must report clean support, sliding and contacts before effort. Whole-trial, last-ten-second and last-five-second total squared torque, absolute torque, time-mean instantaneous RMS torque, and leg/upper-body contributions are compared only on common complete clean trials. Torque is an effort proxy, not electrical energy or a global optimum. Nearby rigid surfaces and two seeds do not demonstrate sitting or leaning anywhere.
